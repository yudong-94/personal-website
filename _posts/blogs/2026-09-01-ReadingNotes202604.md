### Reading List in Past Two Months  

Here are the great articles I came across in the past two months.

#### Data Science, Engineering & Analytics
1. [**The 12 Data Architecture Patterns Every Data Engineer Should Master in 2026**](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/the-12-data-architecture-patterns-every-data-engineer-should-master-in-2026-3598315879c0): Common data architecture patterns with explanation and use cases.  
2. [**The Evolution of Data Teams: How We Moved from ETL to Analytics Engineering**](https://medium.com/@pchandrika0613/the-evolution-of-data-teams-how-we-moved-from-etl-to-analytics-engineering-430ae35f035f): Companies first struggled to collect data (before 2010), then to process data (2010-2020), and now to trust data (2020 onwards) -- that's why we need Analytics Engineers.  
3. [**The Character’s Journey: Designing Content-Specific Analytics for Interactive 3D Environments**](https://medium.com/@AnalyticsAtMeta/the-characters-journey-designing-content-specific-analytics-for-interactive-3d-environments-839da5574d3d): Meta team shares what they learned about designing analytics for interactive 3D environments, including the data architecture behind it and the customer-facing analytics product.  
4. [**The Medallion Architecture Reconsidered: What It Solved, and Where It’s Cracking**](https://medium.com/ai-that-ships/the-medallion-architecture-reconsidered-what-it-solved-and-where-its-cracking-d6073aa1b0fd): Why the medallion architecture — Bronze, Silver, and Gold — mihgt not fit the analytical use cases in the AI era.  
5. [**What an Ontology Actually Is**](https://pub.towardsai.net/what-an-ontology-actually-is-75fc2606c7e8): An ontology is an explicit, machine-readable specification of what exists and what the rules are.  
6. [**The Bronze-Silver-Gold Pattern Is Not Enough: Designing Enterprise Data Products in Databricks**](https://rkamma.medium.com/the-bronze-silver-gold-pattern-is-not-enough-designing-enterprise-data-products-in-databricks-4953bcdae6de): The Medallion architecture is one of the most recognizable Databricks design patterns, but what else is necessary.  


#### Data Career  
1. [**Competencies for Analytics Leadership to Manage Intelligence**](https://eric-sandosham.medium.com/competencies-for-analytics-leadership-to-manage-intelligence-fbfdcee5cadd): 2 additional competencies that need to be mastered by Analytics leaders to succeed in managing intelligence — Systems Thinking and Data Strategy.  
2. [**AI Can Write the Code. These 10 Skills Keep Data Professionals Irreplaceable.**](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/ai-can-write-the-code-these-10-skills-keep-data-professionals-irreplaceable-8cbdb7137beb): AI can write SQL faster than most of us, but it cannot tell you whether the answer is actually right, whether you’re measuring the thing the business cares about, whether a trend is real or a quirk in the data, or whether the question you were asked is even the question you should be answering.  
3. [**Our Data Was Clean. Our Decisions Were Still Wrong. Here’s Why.**](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/why-clean-data-still-leads-to-bad-decisions-the-hidden-data-quality-problem-f640bf4e61bc): The dangerous failures live in data that’s technically accurate and still tells you the wrong story, because of what’s missing, how it’s aggregated, or what changed since it was collected.  



#### AI and LLM
1. [**Data Product Standards Are Moving From Metadata to AI-Ready Context**](https://medium.com/@dr.jarkko.moilanen/data-product-standards-are-moving-from-metadata-to-ai-ready-context-d8a4dc1fe583): Data products need a common language because organizations need data to move across teams, platforms, partners, catalogs, marketplaces, and AI workflows.  
2. [**How We Built DEmate: Taming LLMs for Data Engineering at Meta**](https://medium.com/@AnalyticsAtMeta/how-we-built-demate-taming-llms-for-data-engineering-at-meta-d134e69637c5): DEmate, a specialized AI assistant that doesn’t just write code, but understands the unique topography of Meta’s data engineering ecosystem.  
3. [**6 Data Modeling Mistakes That Trip Up Beginners (Including Me)**](https://medium.com/@srikrishnasm19/6-data-modeling-mistakes-that-trip-up-beginners-including-me-430361da38d1): A good data model starts with the questions the business needs to answer — not with the tables you want to create.  
4. [**How To Build A Reliable AI-Based Structured Data Extraction Pipeline**](https://ai.gopubby.com/building-reliable-ai-document-extraction-pipelines-bbd12dfbe392): A reliable LLM-based extraction system should not only handle the diversity in document formats, but also the differences in organizational requirements. This article walks through an LLM-based extraction pipeline setup with Unstract step by step.  
5. [**Where Agentic AI Actually Helps in Data Engineering**](https://ai.plainenglish.io/where-agentic-ai-actually-helps-in-data-engineering-a3b44fee20d9): The agent was very good at repetitive work — inspecting context, generating SQL, drafting documentation, producing initial tests. What it was not good at was independently knowing whether a business rule had been interpreted correctly.  
6. [**Your Data Engineering Agent Shouldn’t Be Allowed to Touch Production**](https://medium.com/@think-data/your-data-engineering-agent-shouldnt-be-allowed-to-touch-production-944843b9b422): The author stated why they believe we should not give data engineering agent unrestricted production access, no matter how good the demo looks.  
7. [**Anthropic is telling you that Agentic Analytics is not just text-to-SQL](https://medium.com/data-science-collective/anthropic-is-telling-you-that-agentic-analytics-is-not-just-text-to-sql-ce605454bbc9): Anthropic’s own architecture and their stack puts data foundations, sources of truth, skills, and validation ahead of the moment when Claude ever touches a query.  
8. [**Announcing OpenMetadata 2.0: The Open Context Layer for AI Agents**](https://blog.open-metadata.org/announcing-openmetadata-2-0-the-open-context-layer-for-ai-agents-83b8ce8b9dde): The author walks through OpenMetadata 2.0, the next generation of their Open Context Layer for AI agents and data teams.  
9. [**The Semantic Layer, Explained: The Meaning Layer Your AI Agent Needs**](https://medium.com/@kalingwu/semantic-layer-explained-for-ai-agents-be8d2aacd117): A practitioner’s guide to what a semantic layer is, how it works, and why it’s only one of three layers a production analytics agent needs.  
10. [**AI Data Management: What It Actually Takes to Trust an AI Agent**](https://medium.com/@community_md101/ai-data-management-what-it-actually-takes-to-trust-an-ai-agent-c503575f91ff): AI data management is the practice of structuring, governing, and packaging enterprise data so AI systems can consume it reliably at scale.  
11. [**What Is an Analytics Agent and How AI Agents Are Replacing Dashboards**](https://medium.com/@kalingwu/what-is-an-analytics-agent-3f399bb1a24d): What analytics agents actually are, how they differ from copilots and chatbots, and why most of them fail before production.  
12. [**Top 7 Signs Your Data Isn’t Ready for AI**](https://medium.com/@community_md101/top-7-signs-your-data-isnt-ready-for-ai-477c1fdcb089): How to tell if your data is AI-ready from data infra, semantic layer, lineage, access, etc.  
13. [**Are Dashboards Still Relevant in the Age of AI?**](https://eric-sandosham.medium.com/are-dashboards-still-relevant-in-the-age-of-ai-12dffba496ae):  A good dashboard is designed to trigger questions rather than a “collection of answers”, and it is still very useful in the AI era.
