### My Medium Articles!  

Let me start with my new article. I am finally back to writing last month:     
1. [**AI Made Data Scientists Faster. Now It’s Expanding the Job.**](https://towardsdatascience.com/ai-made-data-scientists-faster-now-its-expanding-the-job/): A year ago, I wrote about how AI was changing the day-to-day work of data scientists. At the time, I thought the biggest impact would be straightforward: eliminate low-value work and help us move faster on high-value analysis. A year later, I think I underestimated the change. AI has certainly made data scientists faster—but more importantly, it has expanded the job itself. We can now own more of the end-to-end workflow, take on a broader range of technical work, and delegate more execution to agents. At the same time, judgment, governance, and prioritization are becoming more important, while traditional paths for developing data science skills are being challenged. In this follow-up, I share what has changed in my own work over the past year, what surprised me, and what I think it means for the future of data science.  

### Reading List in Past Two Months  

Here are the great articles I came across in the past two months.

#### Data Science, Engineering & Analytics
1. [**I Tested Google’s TabFM Against XGBoost and AutoGluon**](https://aiadvances.org/i-tested-googles-tabfm-against-xgboost-and-autogluon-ed616c6d085e): Google’s TabFM almost removes task-specific training from the workflow. A detailed comparison betwee TabFM and XGBoost on different use cases.  
2. [**Ontology vs Semantic Layer: Do you need both?**](https://medium.com/@jatin_solanki/ontology-vs-semantic-layer-do-you-need-both-4ebd24a3cad7): The semantic layer governs how the business measures the world. The ontology describes the world being measured.  
3. [**From Gold Tables to Data Products: Redefining the Medallion Contract**](https://medium.com/ai-that-ships/from-gold-tables-to-data-products-redefining-the-medallion-contract-3c55f3740e3a): The boundary between Silver and Gold is ambiguous. A best way to think about is that the Gold layer should also be your data contract with your consumers.  
4. [**Delta Table or Materialized View? Here’s how I actually decide.**](https://medium.com/@savlahanish/delta-table-or-materialized-view-heres-how-i-actually-decide-a049dc307116): Large aggregation refreshed daily from a stable source — MV wins. If CDF is enabled, incremental refresh means only new rows since the last run get processed. Complex transformation with Python UDFs, custom logic, or a streaming source — Delta table, full stop.  
5. [**The Semantic Layer Race: Snowflake, Databricks, Fabric IQ, and Apache Ossie**](https://fred-zhang.medium.com/the-semantic-layer-race-snowflake-databricks-fabric-iq-and-apache-ossie-9d67c5bcd90a): How different products are incorperating semantic layers and how you should get started.  
6. [**Causal Machine Learning: Moving Beyond Prediction to Real Decisions**](https://sciencespectrumu.com/causal-machine-learning-moving-beyond-prediction-to-real-decisions-1fe192b8dcda): A detailed walkthrough of causal ML, common methods, with real code example and pitfalls.  
7. [**The dbt Debate**](https://medium.com/towards-data-engineering/the-dbt-debate-%EF%B8%8F-47ff7478fe27): When a transformation framework still adds value — and when the platform is enough.  
8. [**Are Tabular Foundation Models Ready to Replace Gradient Boosting Models?**](https://pub.towardsai.net/are-tabular-foundation-models-ready-to-replace-gradient-boosting-models-cb039b955162): An introduction of tablular foundation models and compare it with the most common gradient boosting models.  
9. [**We Set Out to Replace Tableau. Four Challenges Later, We Stayed.**](https://medium.com/@officialshutterbug/we-set-out-to-replace-tableau-four-challenges-later-we-stayed-42da724c6c57): A real story of Databricks AI/BI Dashboards and Genie vs. Tableau.
