### ***Carbohydrate Supply***

This week's visualization uses a supplied [FAOSTAT](https://www.fao.org/faostat/en/#data/SUA) export to compare the food sources of carbohydrate supply in 2023. The AI-assisted version shows each food group's share of reported carbohydrate supply across 176 areas; supply measures food availability, not actual intake, and unreported groups are not treated as zero.

#### Insights
* In the supplied 2023 data, cereals and their products are the largest reported carbohydrate source in 168 of 176 areas and provide more than half of reported carbohydrate supply in 99 areas;
* Cereals account for 82.6% of reported carbohydrate supply in Afghanistan and 69.0% in China, mainland, compared with 16.2% in the Democratic Republic of the Congo, showing how strongly the mix of food sources varies across areas.
