### ***Unemployed People by Duration***

This week's visualization is based on [U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics](https://www.bls.gov/opub/ted/2026/1-8-million-people-had-been-unemployed-27-weeks-or-longer-in-july-2026.htm) data and compares the number of unemployed people by duration from July 2016 to July 2026.

#### My Visualization

This visualization compares unemployment-duration groups over time, making the pandemic-era surge and the later rise in long-term unemployment easy to see.

#### Insights
* Short-duration unemployment surged first in spring 2020, while the 27-weeks-or-more group peaked later at 4.2 million in March 2021;
* In July 2026, 1.8 million people had been unemployed for at least 27 weeks, about 60% above the February 2020 level.
