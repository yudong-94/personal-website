### ***Purchasing Power of $100***

This week's visualization is based on [Voronoi](https://www.voronoiapp.com/money/How-Inflation-Has-Eroded-100-Since-2019-by-Country-8915) and compares how much domestic purchasing power remains from $100 at the end of 2019 across 35 countries.

#### My Visualization

This visualization compares the purchasing power remaining across countries, making the effects of cumulative inflation and the gap between the highest- and lowest-retaining currencies easy to see.

#### Insights
* Switzerland retained the most purchasing power at $93.28, while the U.S. retained $77.16;
* Türkiye retained just $10.93, meaning cumulative inflation erased 89.07% of its 2019 purchasing power.
