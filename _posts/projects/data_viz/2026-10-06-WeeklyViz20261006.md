#### Insights
* In the supplied data, California has the highest reported gas price at $6.24, which is $1.76 above the national benchmark and $0.67 above Hawaii, the next-highest state at $5.57;
* Indiana and Texas share the lowest reported price at $3.96, leaving a $2.28 gap between the highest- and lowest-priced states.
