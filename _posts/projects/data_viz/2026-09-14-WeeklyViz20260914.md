### ***The Most Loved and Hated Foods in America***

This week's visualization is based on [YouGov](https://yougov.com/en-us/articles/52689-ranking-americans-most-hated-foods) survey data and compares how U.S. adult citizens feel about 40 foods, from love and like to dislike and hate. The foods were selected based on dislikes reported in an earlier survey.

#### Insights
* Anchovies have the largest combined dislike and hate share at 56%, followed by liver at 54% and sardines at 52%; liver leads on “hate it” alone at 40%;
* Among the 40 foods surveyed, eggs and bananas have the highest combined love and like shares at 84% and 82%; 31 foods receive more positive than negative reactions.
