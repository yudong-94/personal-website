### ***Pets Per Household***

This week's visualization uses pet population data from [World Population Review](https://worldpopulationreview.com/country-rankings/pet-ownership-statistics-by-country) to compare dogs and cats relative to household counts. The AI-assisted version focuses on 26 countries with 2025 Eurostat household estimates; pet-count years are unspecified, so this is a mixed-year comparison of animals per household, not the percentage of households owning pets.

#### Insights
* In the 26-country Plotly comparison, cats outnumber dogs in 16 countries; Austria has the largest cat advantage, with 47.1 cats versus 19.9 dogs per 100 households;
* Slovakia has the largest dog advantage, with 52.8 dogs versus 31.3 cats per 100 households, while Hungary has the highest levels of both species at 69.9 dogs and 59.4 cats per 100 households.
