### ***Who Pays for Healthcare?***

This week's visualization is based on [Voronoi's chart of health care funding by country](https://www.voronoiapp.com/healthcare/Charted-Who-Pays-for-Health-Care-by-Country-9197) and compares how government, patients, other private sources, and external aid fund health spending across 23 selected countries. The article reports 2023 data; government includes social health insurance, while out-of-pocket payments exclude insurance premiums.

#### Insights
* Among the 23 selected countries, Sweden and Japan have the highest government funding shares at 86.1% and 84.8%, while India and Mexico have the highest out-of-pocket shares at 43.9% and 41.2%;
* In the United States, government funds 54.0% of health spending, other private sources fund 35.1%, and patients pay 10.9% out of pocket; these shares describe the funding mix, not the total cost of care.
