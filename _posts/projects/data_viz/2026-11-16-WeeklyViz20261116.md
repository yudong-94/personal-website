### ***Top 10 Names in the U.S.***

This week's visualization uses [U.S. Social Security Administration baby-name data](https://www.ssa.gov/oact/babynames/limits.html) to explore the annual top 10 names for U.S. births from 1880 to 2025. The AI-assisted version focuses on the No. 1 female and male names, showing how long each held the top rank; ranks describe popularity within each year, not shares of births.

#### My Visualization

This visualization explores the top 10 names in the U.S. over time, showing how naming preferences change across years and between female and male births.

#### Insights
* Within 1880–2025, Mary holds the No. 1 female rank for 76 years in total, including a 67-year run from 1880 to 1946 and another nine years from 1953 to 1961;
* John and Michael each hold the No. 1 male rank for 44 years in total; John leads continuously from 1880 to 1923, while Michael leads in 1954–1959 and 1961–1998. Olivia and Liam lead in the final year shown, 2025.
