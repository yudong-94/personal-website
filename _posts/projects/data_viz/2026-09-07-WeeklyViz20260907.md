#### Insights
* San Francisco recorded the largest increase, adding 2,220 average weekday boardings, from 8,788 in August 2025 to 11,008 in August 2026;
* Palo Alto and Sunnyvale had the next-largest gains, adding 760 and 705 average weekday boardings, respectively.
