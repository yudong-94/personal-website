### ***SF Average Rent 2025 vs. 2026***

This week's visualization uses a supplied snapshot of [Zillow Rentals data](https://www.zillow.com/rental-manager/market-trends/san-francisco-ca/) to compare San Francisco rents in 2025 and 2026. The AI-assisted version tracks average monthly rent for studios and one-, two-, and three-bedroom homes from January 2025 through October 2026; changes can reflect the mix of listings as well as price changes.

#### Insights
* In the supplied snapshot, two-bedroom average rent rises from $3,900 in January 2025 to $6,404 in October 2026, a 64.2% increase—the largest percentage gain among the four bedroom categories;
* Over the same period, one-bedroom rent rises 55.0%, three-bedroom rent 48.0%, and studio rent 33.7%; three-bedroom homes remain the most expensive category in October 2026 at $7,550 per month.
