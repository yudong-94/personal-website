### ***Plastic Waste Generated and Recycled***

This week's visualization uses a supplied [Eurostat packaging-waste dataset](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/databrowser/view/env_waspac/default/table) to compare plastic packaging waste generated and recycled in 2024. The AI-assisted version covers 27 reporting countries and shows the EU average separately, estimating recycled and unrecycled kilograms per person from generation and recycling rates.

#### Insights
* Among the 27 reporting countries, Ireland has the highest estimated unrecycled plastic packaging waste at 45.1 kg per person, compared with the EU average of 20.7 kg; Ireland generates 64.9 kg per person and recycles 30.5%;
* Latvia has the lowest estimated unrecycled amount at 7.5 kg per person, with 26.0 kg generated and a 71.0% recycling rate; unrecycled waste includes other treatment routes and should not be interpreted as litter or landfill alone.
