Unemployment by duration

The long-term unemployed numbered 1.8 million in July 2026—far below the pandemic peak, yet about 60% above February 2020.

The pandemic’s delayed shadow: short-duration unemployment surged first in spring 2020; the 27-weeks-or-more group peaked nearly a year later.
October 2025 is unavailable and shown as a gap. Source: U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, The Economics Daily. Data are from the Current Population Survey.

In July 2026, 1.771 million people had been unemployed for at least 27 weeks, compared with 1.110 million in February 2020 and a pandemic-era peak of 4.176 million in March 2021.
