2025 two-bedroom housing wage

The annual income rent demands, state by state

A modest two-bedroom home requires six-figure earnings in California and Hawaii. The highest thresholds cluster along the coasts and Northeast—even though this benchmark assumes housing consumes a full 30% of gross income.
