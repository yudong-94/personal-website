Generative AI use by age

Use falls at every step up the age ladder—from 63.81% of people aged 16–24 to 6.50% of those aged 65–74. The dotted line marks the 32.66% rate for all individuals aged 16–74.

In the EU in 2025, 63.81% of people aged 16–24 used generative AI tools in the previous three months, compared with 6.50% of people aged 65–74. The all-individuals rate was 32.66%.
